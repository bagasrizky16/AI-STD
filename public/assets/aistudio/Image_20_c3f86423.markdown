## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies authentic Indonesian village hospitality, sustainable agro-tourism, and rich volcanic highlands biodiversity. The target audience encompasses domestic weekend holidaymakers from major cities, international eco-conscious travelers, educational school groups, and cultural preservation enthusiasts seeking restorative retreats.

The emotional signature is **warm, welcoming, grounding, and deeply connected to nature**. The visual style blends Modern Organic Tourism with Tactile Craft Minimalism:
- Immersive high-contrast natural photography showcasing terraced rice paddies, morning mountain mist, and village artisan crafts.
- Clean, open, breathable layouts anchored by warm earth tones and rich tropical foliage greens.
- Generous touch ergonomics tailored for outdoor mobile use under bright sunlight.
- Grounded visual reassurance reflecting community-owned, trustworthy rural hospitality.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from Mount Merapi/Merbabu foothills—volcanic soil, lush tropical canopies, and morning sunlight on teak terraces.

- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Highland Pine. Used for critical navigation headers, primary headlines, strong accents, and grounding structural containers. Lighter tonal variants (`#2D6A4F`, `#40916C`) serve active interactive states and badges.
- **Secondary (`#E07A5F`)**: Terracotta Clay. Signifies warmth, cultural festivities, village craft markets, highlight chips, and promotional banners.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`)**: Direct Messaging Green. Reserved exclusively for direct WhatsApp customer service inquiries, instant reservation flows, and real-time host chat.
- **Neutral (`#263228`)**: Deep Peat. A softened charcoal-green for high-contrast, fatigue-free body text.
- **Surfaces & Canvases**: The foundational canvas uses Morning Mist Cream (`#FAFAF7`), with surface cards elevated in pure clean white (`#FFFFFF`) to make photography and booking specs pop vibrantly.

## Typography

The design system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Conceived and designed in Indonesia, it pairs modern geometric clarity with warm humanist open counters that ensure crisp legibility on low-cost mobile displays under bright outdoor light.

- **Display & Headlines**: Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) provide an inviting yet authoritative editorial framing for Indonesian phrases (*"Jelajahi Desa"*, *"Paket Homestay"*).
- **Price Display**: Numerical currency figures (e.g., `Rp 150.000`) use tabular figures with `label-lg` or `headline-md` weights for rapid scanning.
- **Body & Captions**: Generous line height ensures effortless reading for multi-paragraph cultural guides and activity itineraries.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout architecture that smoothly expands to a centered desktop canvas with a maximum width of `1200px`.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `1rem` gutters. All primary action components have a minimum vertical touch height of `48px`. The lower `80px` of the viewport is protected by dynamic safe-area padding for the persistent WhatsApp booking bar.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. Activity listings transition into 2-column card grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with up to `2.5rem` outer margins. Booking summaries transform into an anchored sticky right-sidebar card, disabling the bottom mobile sheet.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic grays in favor of soft, sunlight-filtered ambient drop shadows warmed with low-opacity umber:

- **Flat / Ground Tier**: Canvas background `#FAFAF7` sits beneath base surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Cards, List Elements)**: White background `#FFFFFF` with outline `1px solid rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)` and shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(60, 50, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Headers, Interactive Modals, Active Filters)**: Shadow `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(60, 50, 40, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Mobile Sticky WhatsApp Bar & Overlays)**: Shadow `0 -4px 20px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)` to establish clear elevation above scrollable content.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, reflecting the organic contours of natural landscapes, river stones, and terraced slopes.

- Buttons and standard input fields use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Cards, modal containers, and photo hero frames use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Tags, status pills, and the WhatsApp sticky floating trigger utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`) to create distinction against structured content boxes.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Bottom Booking Bar (Mobile Exclusive)
- **Container**: Pinned to bottom viewport, surface `#FFFFFF`, border-top `1px solid rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.1)`, `0 -4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` shadow.
- **Content**: Left side stacks the price preview (`body-sm` "Mulai dari", `headline-sm` "Rp 150.000/org"). Right side features the WhatsApp CTA.
- **WhatsApp Button**: Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `700`, inline WhatsApp icon, minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `1.25rem`, pill rounded (`9999px`).

### 2. Destination & Package Cards
- **Hero Image**: Aspect ratio `16:9` (mobile) or `4:3` (grid), `overflow: hidden`, `rounded-t-lg`. Displays overlay badges for categories (e.g., "Ekowisata", "Homestay", "Agro").
- **Card Body**: Pure white background, `1rem` padding, containing title (`headline-sm`), brief descriptive text (`body-sm`), operational hours, and an explicit price tag.

### 3. Badges and Chips
- **Operational Badge**: Open status utilizes `#2D6A4F` text on `#E8F5E9` background. Closed/Seasonal uses `#E07A5F` on `#FDF2E9`.
- **Price Badge**: Highlighted with soft Terracotta (`#E07A5F`) or Forest Green (`#1B4332`), bold tabular font, accompanied by clear terms (e.g., `Rp 25.000 / tiket masuk`).

### 4. Inputs & Form Fields
- Minimal height `48px` to ensure effortless field input on touchscreens.
- Border `1.5px solid rgba(38, 50, 40, 0.2)`, active state changes border to `#2D6A4F` with an outer ring glow of `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`.

### 5. Host & Community Story Avatars
- Rounded circular frame (`9999px`) with `2px solid #E07A5F` border, pairing human face photography with verified village resident badges.
